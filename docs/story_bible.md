# Paws of Destiny — Story Bible

## Working Title
**Paws of Destiny: The Quest for the Dog King**

## Target Audience
Elementary school children (ages 6-11)

## Moral/Theme
Don't judge others by their reputation. Talk to people before assuming the worst.
Secondary: Teamwork, sharing, and kindness solve more problems than fighting.

---

## World: Whiskeria

A colorful land of rolling meadows, dark forests, snowy peaks, and coastal towns — all inhabited by cats, with a mysterious Dog King ruling from a distant fortress.

## Story Arc

### Act 1 — "The Call" (Zones 1)
- Squirrels steal fish from Purrville
- Grandma Mittens reveals the Puzzle Map (7 pieces)
- Whiskers learns his abilities and finds Map Piece 1

### Act 2 — "The Journey" (Zones 2-6)
- Each zone has a map piece and (most) a new cat friend
- NPCs share increasingly absurd Dog King rumors
- Team grows, abilities combine for puzzles

### Act 3 — "The Reveal" (Zone 7)
- Reach the fortress — it's enormous and scary
- The Dog King is a tiny Chihuahua named King Biscuit
- He didn't order the stealing — his critter army acted alone because they were hungry
- Resolution: everyone works together to create a shared food market

## Hunger/Thirst System

| Activity | Hunger Drain | Thirst Drain |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| Idle | 1x (very slow) | 1x |
| Walking | 2x | 1.5x |
| Running | 4x | 3x |
| Using abilities | 3x burst | 2x burst |

- At 25%: visual warning (gray screen edges, sad meow)
- At 0%: cat sits down, won't move — NOT game over, just frozen until fed
- No permadeath — this is a kids' game
