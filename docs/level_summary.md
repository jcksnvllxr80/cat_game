# Paws of Destiny — Level Summary

Complete breakdown of what each level contains: mechanics, enemies, NPCs, collectibles, puzzles, and key events.

---

## Zone 1: Purrville Meadows (Level1Scene)

**World:** 4000 x 576

**New Abilities:** Pounce Attack (X key)

**Party at Start:** Whiskers only

**Platforms:** Grass/dirt ground, floating platforms, 2 pit gaps, breakable crates (5 total)

**Enemies:**
- 6 Squirrels (ground patrol, 10 damage)

**NPCs:**
1. Mr. Pawston (x:250) — reports squirrels stealing tuna, sends you to Grandma Mittens
2. Grandma Mittens (x:1500) — reveals the Puzzle Map quest, hints at map piece in her attic
3. Sgt. Fluffbottom (x:3200) — Dog King rumor: "ate an entire fishing boat!", gives Fish Pendant

**Collectibles:**
- 16 tuna cans scattered throughout
- 8 water bowls
- Fish Pendant (near Sgt. Fluffbottom) — reduces food drain by 25%
- Map Piece 1/7 (x:1850, elevated platform near Grandma Mittens)

**Key Events:**
- Intro dialog: Whiskers wakes up, hears crash outside
- Fish Pendant acquisition
- First map piece discovery

**Exit:** Far right, transitions to Level 2 (Whispering Woods). Swaps to level2music.

**Controls Introduced:** Arrow keys / A+D movement, SHIFT run, SPACE jump, X pounce, E talk

---

## Zone 2: Whispering Woods (Level2Scene)

**World:** 5000 x 576

**New Abilities:** Night Vision (V key), Character Switching (TAB)

**Party at Start:** Whiskers (Luna joins mid-level)

**Platforms:** Dark forest ground, floating platforms, 3 pit gaps, breakable crates (6 total)

**Special Mechanics:**
- 3 Dark Zones (x:1800, x:3200, x:4200) — pitch black, alpha 0.85 overlay
- Hidden paths — invisible platforms (alpha 0) revealed only by Luna's Night Vision (alpha 0.7, green tint)
- Night Vision drains extra thirst (+0.3/sec)

**Enemies:**
- 6 Raccoons (ground patrol, 10 damage)
- 6 Crows (flying sine wave pattern, 10 damage)

**NPCs:**
1. Frightened Mouse (x:200) — Dog King rumor: "LASER EYES!", hints about trapped cat near boulders
2. Professor Hoot (x:1500) — hints about hidden paths and special sight
3. Wandering Cat (x:3800) — hints about Great Tree and invisible paths

**Luna Rescue Puzzle:**
- 3 boulders blocking cave entrance (x:920-960)
- Each boulder takes 3 pounce hits to break
- Luna NPC behind boulders (barely visible, alpha 0.4)
- Luna cry for help text pulses above boulders
- When all boulders cleared, Luna rescued and joins party
- Dialog: Luna explains Night Vision, how to switch characters

**Collectibles:**
- 18 tuna cans
- 11 water bowls
- Map Piece 2/7 (x:4500, inside Great Tree — hidden, only visible with Night Vision)

**Ambient:** Fireflies pulse in dark zones, fog/mist layers, decorative trees, Great Tree landmark

**Exit:** Far right, transitions to Level 3 (Tuna Bay Docks). Swaps to level3music.

**Controls Introduced:** TAB switch character, V night vision

---

## Zone 3: Tuna Bay Docks (Level3Scene)

**World:** 5500 x 576

**New Abilities:** Boots' Super Sprint (W key — 3x speed, 3s burst, 5s cooldown)

**Party at Start:** Whiskers, Luna (Boots joins mid-level)

**Platforms:** Wooden dock planks, floating crates/driftwood over water gaps, wider water gaps than previous levels

**Special Mechanics:**
- Water hazard — falling below y:540 deals 15 damage (more than pits, cats hate water)
- "Cats don't like water!" message on water fall

**Enemies:**
- 6 Crabs (ground patrol, 10 damage)
- 7 Seagulls (flying sine wave, 10 damage)

**NPCs:**
1. Captain Saltwhisker (x:300) — Dog King rumor: shadow blocked sun for 3 days, bark created tidal wave. Mentions Boots stuck on island
2. Dock Worker Cat (x:1500) — Dog King rumor: army of ten thousand
3. Fish Merchant Cat (x:4000) — mentions sunken ship has a map piece

**Boots Rescue:**
- Boots NPC at x:2750 on a small island platform surrounded by water gaps
- Pulsing help text above him
- When player reaches within 60px, rescue dialog triggers
- Boots joins party — explains Super Sprint ability
- Dialog: Boots was stuck for a week doing push-ups, can run across water in short bursts

**Collectibles:**
- 20 tuna cans
- 12 water bowls
- Map Piece 3/7 (x:5000, elevated platform near sunken ship area)

**Ambient:** Coastal parallax background, ocean horizon, dock posts, buoys, anchor decorations

**Exit:** Far right, labeled "To Catnip Canyon". Swaps to level4music.

**Controls Introduced:** W key for Boots' Super Sprint

## Zone 4: Catnip Canyon (Level4Scene)

**World:** 6000 x 576

**New Abilities:** Cleo's Wall Climb (press UP while against a wall as Cleo)

**Party at Start:** Whiskers, Luna, Boots (Cleo joins mid-level)

**Platforms:** Canyon rock platforms, vertical layout with tall columns, canyon crevasse gaps

**Special Mechanics:**
- Wall climb: when Cleo is active and player is against a wall (blocked left/right), pressing UP allows vertical climbing

**Enemies:**
- 7 Snakes (ground patrol, 10 damage)
- 9 Hawks (flying sine wave, 10 damage)

**NPCs:**
1. Shopkeeper Cat (x:250) — Dog King rumor: "army of TEN THOUSAND dogs!"
2. Traveling Cat (x:400) — "Ten thousand? I heard it was a MILLION. And they all breathe fire."
3. Kit the Kitten (x:500) — "My big brother says the Dog King can fly."
4. Canyon Guide Cat (x:2000) — hints about Cleo stuck up high, mentions map piece at summit

**Cleo Rescue Puzzle:**
- 3 vine roots at ground level (x:3100, 3200, 3300) — pounce to cut
- Cleo NPC at x:3200, y:150 (high up on cliff)
- Pulsing cry for help text
- Track vinesCut counter — when all 3 cut, Cleo is freed
- Dialog: Cleo was climbing and got tangled, once stared down a German Shepherd
- Cleo joins party

**Collectibles:**
- 18 tuna cans
- 12 water bowls
- Map Piece 4/7 (x:5500, y:100 — very high platform at canyon summit)

**Ambient:** Warm orange/red sunset sky, layered canyon walls, cacti, dust haze

**Exit:** Far right, labeled "To The Yarn Factory". Swaps to level5music.

## Zone 5: The Yarn Factory (Level5Scene)

**World:** 6000 x 576

**New Abilities:** None (puzzle-focused, uses all existing abilities)

**Party at Start:** Whiskers, Luna, Boots, Cleo (no new recruit)

**Platforms:** Metal grating platforms, dark metal floor, conveyor belt sections

**Special Mechanics:**
- Conveyor belts — zones that push the player left or right when standing on them
- 3 switches scattered through level (x:1500, x:2500, x:4000) — pounce to activate
- Control room wall blocks map piece until all 3 switches activated
- Yarn balls — rolling hazards that patrol platforms (5 damage + knockback, destroyable by pounce)

**Enemies:**
- 7 Factory Rats (ground patrol, 10 damage)
- 6 Mechanical Spiders (ceiling enemies, drop when player is within 150px, 10 damage)

**NPCs:**
1. Old Factory Cat (x:400) — factory used to make finest yarn, then Dog King's minions took over
2. Maintenance Mouse (x:3000) — explains 3 switches needed to unlock control room. Dog King rumor: "sneezed and caused an earthquake!"

**Puzzle: 3 Switches**
- Switch 1 at x:1500, Switch 2 at x:2500, Switch 3 at x:4000
- Each changes from red (switch_off) to green (switch_on) when pounced
- switchesHit counter tracks progress
- HUD shows switch count
- When all 3 hit: "Control room unlocked!" message, blocking wall removed with fade animation
- Map piece accessible in control room area

**Collectibles:**
- 20 tuna cans
- 12 water bowls
- Map Piece 5/7 (x:5200, in control room — only accessible after all 3 switches)

**Ambient:** Industrial parallax, factory silhouettes with chimneys, smoke, pipes, gears

**Intro Dialog:**
- Luna: "This place gives me the creeps."
- Cleo: "Someone who doesn't want visitors."
- Boots: "Is it weird that I really want to chase that yarn?"

**Exit:** Far right, labeled "To Snowpaw Summit". Swaps to level6music.

## Zone 6: Snowpaw Summit (Level6Scene)

**World:** 6500 x 576

**New Abilities:** Mochi's Belly Bounce (W key — super high jump, -700 velocity vs normal -400)

**Party at Start:** Whiskers, Luna, Boots, Cleo (Mochi joins mid-level)

**Platforms:** Snow/ice platforms, 3 crevasse gaps

**Special Mechanics:**
- Ice zones — 4 slippery areas where player slides (0.3x deceleration instead of instant stop)
- Cold environment — hunger drains faster (+0.2/sec)
- Falling snowflakes (40 ambient particles)

**Enemies:**
- 7 Snow Foxes (ground patrol, 10 damage)
- 7 Ice Bats (flying sine wave, 10 damage)

**NPCs:**
1. Shivering Cat (x:300) — Dog King rumor: "throne made of BONES, sleeps on LAVA!"
2. Mountain Goat (x:3500) — hints about Mochi stuck in a hole 500 steps east

**Mochi Rescue Puzzle:**
- Mochi NPC at x:4200, wedged in a hole in the snow
- Player needs 5 tuna collected in this level (tunasThisLevel counter)
- If approaching with fewer, hint message shown
- After feeding 5 tuna, Mochi wiggles out
- Dialog: Mochi calls being stuck "committed to this location", demonstrates Belly Bounce
- Dog King rumor: "a MUSTACHE made of FIRE"
- Mochi joins party

**Collectibles:**
- 22 tuna cans (extra — need 5 for Mochi rescue)
- 12 water bowls
- Map Piece 6/7 (x:5800, y:150 — high platform, reachable with Mochi's Belly Bounce)

**Ambient:** Pale blue-white sky, snow-capped peaks, pine trees, constant falling snowflakes, mist

**Intro Dialog:**
- Cleo: "I HATE the cold. My fur is too thin for this."
- Boots: "Just run faster! That warms you up!"
- Cleo: "Not all of us solve every problem by running, Boots."

**Exit:** Far right, labeled "To Dog King's Fortress". Swaps to level7music.

## Zone 7: Dog King's Fortress (Level7Scene)

**World:** 7000 x 576

**New Abilities:** None (all abilities required to navigate)

**Party at Start:** Whiskers, Luna, Boots, Cleo, Mochi (full party)

**Platforms:** Dark stone brick platforms, cobblestone ground, 3 lava pit gaps

**Special Mechanics:**
- 2 Dark Zones requiring Luna's Night Vision (same system as Level 2)
- Hidden paths visible only with Night Vision
- All 5 cat abilities needed to progress:
  - Dark zones (Luna's Night Vision)
  - Water/speed gaps (Boots' Sprint)
  - High walls (Cleo's Wall Climb)
  - High platforms (Mochi's Belly Bounce)
  - Breakable walls/crates (Whiskers' Pounce)
- Enemies deal 15 damage (tougher than all previous zones)

**Enemies:**
- 8 Fortress Guards (armored dogs, ground patrol, 15 damage)
- 8 Fortress Bats (flying sine wave, 15 damage)

**NPCs:**
1. Fortress Mouse (x:600) — final Dog King rumor: "throne made of BONES, sleeps on LAVA!"
2. King Biscuit (x:5500) — the Dog King himself, a tiny shivering chihuahua with an oversized crown

**Key Events:**

*Opening:*
- Luna: "It's... it's real."
- Mochi: "I'm a little scared. But mostly hungry."

*Map Piece 7/7 (x:2000):*
- "The last piece! The Puzzle Map is complete!"
- Map points to the throne room

*Throne Room Encounter (x:5500):*
- Triggered when player gets within 100px of King Biscuit
- The big reveal: King Biscuit is a tiny chihuahua, no bigger than a kitten
- He didn't order the stealing — his critters acted alone because they were hungry
- King Biscuit cries tiny chihuahua tears
- The team realizes he's telling the truth

*Resolution:*
- Whiskers proposes sharing instead of stealing
- King Biscuit is overjoyed at the idea of friends

*Ending Narration (Grandma Mittens):*
- "They did something much braver — they listened."
- "The scariest monster is usually just someone who needs a friend."

*THE END Screen:*
- Fade to black
- "THE END" and "Paws of Destiny" title
- "Thank you for playing!"
- Click to return to Title Screen

**Collectibles:**
- 20 tuna cans
- 12 water bowls
- Map Piece 7/7 (x:2000, y:300 — just inside the fortress gates)

**Ambient:** Dark stormy sky, massive stone walls, iron gates, torch flames, towers with battlements, warm glow near throne room

**No Exit:** Game ends in the throne room with finale sequence, returns to TitleScene.

## Controls Reference (All Levels)

| Key | Action | Available |
|-----|--------|-----------|
| Arrow Keys / A+D | Move left/right | All levels |
| SHIFT | Run (1.6x speed) | All levels |
| SPACE | Jump | All levels |
| X | Pounce Attack | All levels |
| E | Talk to NPC | All levels |
| TAB | Switch Character | Level 2+ (when party > 1) |
| V | Toggle Night Vision (Luna only) | Level 2, 7 |
| W | Boots Sprint / Mochi Belly Bounce | Level 3+ / Level 6+ |
| M | Volume Down | All levels |
| N | Volume Up | All levels |
